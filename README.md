A simple kinetic solver that tracks the depletion of oleic acid (aerosol) with ozone. 

Shows how the radius of the aerosol changes as the reaction progresses.

Can be updated for ODE instead of first-order kinetics. 
Further Work: include reformation of aerosol - Aerosol wouldn't just continually deplete over time reacting with one reactant, other factors will contribute to the aerosol growing and other reactants may cause it to further decrease.
Would be interesting to see how the spherical shape of the aerosol affects kinetics - rahter than assuming the same kinetics found in solution.
